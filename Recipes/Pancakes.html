Pancakes

Description

Soft and fluffy pancakes for breakfast. Kids love it.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 spoon suger
  • Butter
  • Honey

Steps

  1. Mix flour, egg, milk and suger in a bowl.
  2. Heat pan with little butter.
  3. Pour butter and cook 2 minutes each side.
  4. Serve with honey.
