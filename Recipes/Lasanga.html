Lasanga

Description

This is a simple and tasty lasanga. It has layers of pasta, sauce, and cheese. It is perfect for family dinner.

You make it in about one hour. Even beginners can make it.

Ingredients

  • Lasanga Sheets
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Minced Beef or Chicken
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Mozarella Cheese
  • Salt and Pepper

Steps

  1. Heat the pan and cook onion and garlic.
  2. Add minced meat and cook until brown.
  3. Add tomato sauce, salt and pepper. Cook for 10 10 minutes.
  4. In a tray, put one layer of lasanga sheets, then sauce, then cheese.
  5. Repeat layers 3 times.
  6. Bake in oven at 180C for 30 minutes.
  7. Let it cool for 5 minutes and serve.
