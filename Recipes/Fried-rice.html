Fried Rice

Description

Quick and easy fried rice. Best way to use leftover rice

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 2 eggs
  • Carrot
  • Spring onion
  • Soy sauce
  • Garlic
  • Oil and salt

Steps

  1. Heat oil in pan.
  2. Fry garlic and carrot.
  3. Add eggs and scramble.
  4. Add rice and soy sauce.
  5. Add spring onion and serve hot.
